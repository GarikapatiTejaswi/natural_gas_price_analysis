# Natural Gas Price Analysis and Forecasting

## Project Overview
This project analyzes historical natural gas prices to identify trends and seasonal variations. It also estimates prices for specific dates and forecasts prices for one additional year using Python.

## Internship
**Organization:** DraconX  
**Task:** Internship Task 01 – Natural Gas Price Analysis and Forecasting

## Objectives
- Analyze historical monthly natural gas prices.
- Identify seasonal patterns and monthly price variations.
- Estimate prices for any requested date.
- Forecast prices for one additional year beyond the historical dataset.
- Visualize historical trends and forecasted prices.

## Dataset
- Historical period: October 2020 – September 2024
- Frequency: Monthly
- Forecast period: October 2024 – September 2025

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Scikit-learn

## Methodology
1. Load and inspect the dataset.
2. Clean and prepare the date and price columns.
3. Visualize historical price movements.
4. Analyze average prices by month to explore seasonality.
5. Implement date-based price estimation.
6. Forecast prices for the next 12 months.
7. Plot and interpret the results.

## Visualizations
The project includes:
- Historical natural gas price trend.
- Average price by calendar month.
- Historical prices compared with forecasted prices.

## How to Run
1. Open the notebook in Google Colab.
2. Upload the required CSV dataset if it is not included in the repository.
3. Run the notebook cells in order.
4. Use the price estimation function to estimate prices for a requested date.

## Limitations
The forecast is based on historical price patterns and does not account for all market factors, such as weather, supply and demand changes, storage levels, or geopolitical events. Forecast values are estimates, not guaranteed future market prices.
